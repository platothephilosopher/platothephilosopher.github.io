Welcome, wanderer of this world,

you have found yourself on a website born from the hope that it may be visited by people who have been, or will yet be, touched by Plato’s philosophy and the corresponding way of life.

Besides a brief introduction and a book which I have composed for myself and for all of us from quotations related to this matter, you will find here, if thou desirest, for download what may aid you on your own journey toward happiness, where clearly each of us walks.

For I think that Homer was very right in saying that
When two go together, one sees before the other, for all men who have a companion are readier in deed, word, or thought; but if a man
Sees a thing when he is alone, he goes about straightway seeking until he finds some one to whom he may show his discoveries, and who may confirm him in them.

(Plato, dialogue Protagoras, translated by BJ)
